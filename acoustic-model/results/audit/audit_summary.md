## Numbering scheme note

dementia/ filenames use a suffix marker approximating years relative to symptom onset (observed values: 0, 2, 5, 10, 15), sometimes with a second sub-index for multiple clips at the same marker.
nodementia/ and nodementia2/ filenames use a suffix marker that is always 1, 2, or 3 - an index into DementiaNet's 5/10/15-years-before-death-or-current-age scheme, not a literal year value.
These are two unrelated numbering schemes and must not be conflated when this data is used for splitting or feature engineering later.
