## Channel-quality confound check

Proxies averaged per subject first (215 subjects), then tested against the label. Bonferroni across the 3 proxies, separately for each test. Rank-biserial > 0 means higher in the dementia group.

| proxy | median dementia | median no dementia | point-biserial r | p (Bonf.) | rank-biserial | MWU p (Bonf.) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| noise_floor_dbfs | -49.146 | -51.0631 | 0.1613 | 0.0538 | 0.1638 | 0.1291 |
| bandwidth_hz | 4141.8784 | 4401.9964 | -0.0731 | 0.8584 | -0.1065 | 0.5649 |
| noise_flatness | 0.0021 | 0.0033 | -0.045 | 1.0 | -0.2888 | 0.0011 |

Caveat: the noise floor and noise flatness are measured on each file's quietest frames, which are mostly pauses, so they are partly coupled to how much the speaker pauses (PLAN.md 3.3).
