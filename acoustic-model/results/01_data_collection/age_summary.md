# Stage 1 - Age at recording (decision D6)

Generated by `scripts/01b_age_at_recording.py` from DementiaNet's metadata sheet and the clip-name suffixes. DementiaNet records no recording dates, so every age is a range.

## How the clip suffix maps to time

- Dementia `_0/_5/_10/_15`: after symptom onset; 0-5, 5-10, 10-15 years before onset (sheet columns 'URLs after symptoms', '5 years', '5 < 10 years', '10 < 15 years').
- No dementia `_1/_2/_3`: <55 / 55-70 / >70. Established from the data: of 61 controls in the sheet, this ordering matches the URL columns exactly for 55 and as a subset for 60; the next-best ordering matches 41 exactly (`control_suffix_order_evidence.csv`). The README describes these buckets two inconsistent ways (5/10/15 years before death or current age, and >70 / 55-70 / <55), and the sheet's column headers are the age bands.
- Clip suffixes seen: dementia {'5': 44, '10': 32, '0': 27, '15': 27, '2': 1}, no dementia {'3': 126, '2': 100, '1': 98}.

## Coverage

- Dementia: 84 subjects, 84 in the sheet; 123 of 131 clips have a bounded age (unbounded = missing onset year or an unknown suffix).
- No dementia: 131 subjects, only 61 in the sheet. The age band still follows from the suffix for every control clip, assuming the suffix convention confirmed on the sheet's controls holds for the rest (same naming scheme, same dataset).
- Upper bounds for living people use 2021, the latest death year in the sheet (the dataset has no date).

## Age band at recording, by class

A dementia clip gets a band only if its whole age range falls inside it; 'straddles' means it crosses 55 or 70.
